Why it feels alive

More than a wallpaper

Every SoundMural visualization is driven by the actual audio — real samples from your music, not a clock. Bass, mid, treble, onsets, and the beat each steering their own part of the picture.

A fractal that breathes with the drop

The signature effect is a Julia fractal, connected if and only if its constant lies within the Mandelbrot set. Bass energy drives that constant toward the boundary — so the mural strays apart on heavy bass, shatters into dust on a genuine drop, then reforms intact.

Turn down the camera drift and pace with Reduce Motion — without ever silencing the music's own authority over the visuals.

Questions

Everything you might ask

Which Apple TVs does it run on?
SoundMural needs tvOS 26 and is tuned for Apple TV 4K (3rd generation), holding 1080p60 with adaptive quality. If your Apple TV can't run tvOS 26, it won't install — check the App Store entry for your device.
Which music sources does it support?
Plex, Jellyfin, and Navidrome feed the visualizer real audio samples — that's what makes the fractal genuinely react to the music. Spotify is supported in metadata mode: it shows now-playing, artwork, and transport, but DRM prevents access to Spotify's audio, so those visuals run on a synthetic driver rather than real samples.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Anything you download to the TV plays fully offline. Streaming from your own server works across your home network (or anywhere the server is reachable). SoundMural itself needs no cloud service.
Does it collect or share any of my data?
No. Connections to your music server are TLS-first, nothing you play is uploaded, and SoundMural collects no telemetry and tracks no data. Spotify requires the system's standard browser sign-in, exactly like the OS music app.
How does the fractal actually react to music?
The signature effect is a Julia fractal whose constant is pushed toward the Mandelbrot boundary by bass energy. While the set stays connected, the mural breathes intact through most of a track; on a heavy drop it strains apart; on a genuine drop it shatters into disconnected dust — then reforms. Mid, treble, onsets, and the beat steer different parts of each other visualization.
Is there really no subscription?
Correct. The app is free to try, and a single $9.99 one-time in-app purchase unlocks every visualization across all five packs, including free updates, on every Apple TV you own. No recurring charge, ever.
I have Reachability / accessibility needs — what's available?
Reduce Motion damps camera drift and pace without muting the music's influence on the visuals. Visualization order and packs are fully switchable, and the on-screen player keeps controls within the standard tvOS focus model.
