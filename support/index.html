Play radio without an account

  1. Choose Internet Radio from the music-source chooser. If you already use another source, open Settings → Music Source → Internet Radio.
  2. Open Stations and select a station, or use the Search tab to find one.
  3. Select the station to start playback and its visualization. Use the remote's Back/Menu button to return from playback to browsing.

Radio needs an internet connection, but no Plex server, music subscription or SoundMural account. Stations are run by independent broadcasters; a station may be unavailable, change its stream, or restrict where it can be heard. If one fails, try another before changing your setup.

Using an older TestFlight beta? Build 20 and other versions without the Share Station Selections setting may automatically report your station selection to radio-browser.info when you tune. To avoid those reports, update to a version with the control and leave it off, or do not use radio in the older version. Read the reporting details.

Connect your music

Choose your service from the source chooser, or open Settings → Music Source. You need access to the selected service or server; SoundMural does not create that account for you.

Plex

Follow the on-screen link-code instructions to authorize SoundMural with your Plex account, then choose a server with a music library. Keep the server online and reachable from your Apple TV. To choose a different server while Plex is active, use Settings → Plex Server.

Jellyfin

Enter your Jellyfin server address and select Connect. If Quick Connect is available, open your user menu in the Jellyfin app or website, choose Quick Connect, and enter the code shown on the TV. If the server instead asks for credentials, use its Jellyfin username and password.

Navidrome

Enter the Navidrome server address, username and password, then select Sign In. Include a port or path if your server requires one. Use the account for that server, not your Apple Account.

Apple Music

Choose Apple Music and select Allow Access when offered. It uses this Apple TV's Apple Account and requires access to Apple Music catalog playback through an eligible subscription. If access was denied, manage the app's music access in tvOS Settings and return to SoundMural. Follow any subscription or authorization message shown in the app.

Apple Music visuals are track-informed. Apple Music does not expose PCM audio samples to SoundMural, so this is not waveform or spectrum analysis. Plex, Jellyfin, Navidrome and Internet Radio supply audio the app can analyse on the Apple TV.

If a server will not connect

Check the address, port and credentials, and confirm the server is running. For a home server, make sure the Apple TV can reach it on your network. Prefer an HTTPS address. HTTP connections are not encrypted; for Jellyfin, Quick Connect avoids sending your password through the password form, but it does not make an HTTP connection encrypted.

Free visuals and the Launch Collection

At launch, Julia, Aurora, Starfield, Breaker and Plasma will be free, with unlimited listening from Plex, Jellyfin, Navidrome, Apple Music and Internet Radio. There is no trial timer. Provider subscriptions and server access are separate; Internet Radio needs no SoundMural account or music subscription.

The optional US $9.99 one-time Launch Collection purchase will unlock the exact 26 launch visuals shown on the homepage, including the free five, and updates to those visuals. Future collections will be sold separately. There is no SoundMural subscription. The app will display Apple's localized price before purchase.

Purchases will become available with the App Store release. SoundMural uses your Apple Account for purchases and restoration, with automatic access restoration and an explicit Restore Purchases action in Settings. An older beta may not have these controls. If access is missing in a build that includes purchases, check that you are using the purchasing Apple Account and use Restore Purchases.

A store connection or product-loading error should not require another purchase. A pending purchase needs Apple's approval before the collection becomes available; a cancelled purchase leaves the five free visuals and playback available. Contact support with the exact message and build number if you need help.

Playback and visualizations

No sound or playback stopped

Check the TV or receiver's volume and audio output. Try another track or station, then confirm the music server or internet connection is available. If the app shows an error, include its exact text when contacting support.

A radio station will not skip tracks

Radio is a live stream. There is no next track for SoundMural to select; choose another station instead.

The motion is too strong

Enable Reduce Motion in tvOS Settings. SoundMural honors that preference to reduce camera drift and motion. You can also adjust visualizer settings in the app. Stop viewing if an effect is uncomfortable; reduced motion does not remove every moving or flashing element.

Find your version

Open Settings → About in SoundMural for the version and build. Controls and behavior can differ between beta builds, so include both numbers in your report.

Privacy and disconnecting sources

In versions that include it, open Settings → About → Privacy Policy → Share Station Selections. This switch is off by default and applies only to this Apple TV. Turning it off stops future selection reports without disabling radio search or playback. It does not erase requests already sent. Radio search and streaming still connect to the directory and station services.

To sign out of Plex, Jellyfin or Navidrome, open Settings → Music Source, press and hold the connected source, and select Sign Out. Apple Music authorization is managed through tvOS Settings rather than an in-app Sign Out button.

To remove local app settings and cached files, sign out of connected sources first, then delete SoundMural from your Apple TV. The app has no single erase-all-data button. Deleting it does not necessarily remove Keychain credentials, iCloud-synced preferences or records held by music services. Read the full data-management details.

Send a useful support request

Email hello@soundmural.com with:

  • The SoundMural version and build from Settings → About, and whether you installed it through TestFlight.
  • Your Apple TV model and tvOS version.
  • The music source, plus the station or track if relevant.
  • The steps that led to the issue, what you expected, and what happened.
  • The exact error message, or an optional screenshot with private details removed.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, authorization codes or private server links. A screenshot or diagnostic file can contain personal information; review it before sending. By emailing, you share your email address and message with support. See the Privacy Policy for details.
